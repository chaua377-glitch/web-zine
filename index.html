SEVENTEEN

Who?

Seventeen is a South Korean K-pop boy band. They have a total of 13 members namely:

  1. S.coups
  2. Jeonghan
  3. Joshua
  4. Jun
  5. Hoshi
  6. Wonwoo
  7. Woozi
  8. DK
  9. Mingyu
  10. The8
  11. Seungkwan
  12. Vernon
  13. Dino

They debuted on May 26, 2015, with the single "Adore U". They are a self made group as they have written and produced their own music as well as choreographed their dances. The group has three units- the vocal unit, the hip hop unit and the performance unit. THe leaders of the vocal unit and performance unit are Woozi and Hoshi. S.coups is the leader of the hip hop unit and the general leader of seventeen. 13 members+ 3units+1 team= 17 -this is how the name Seventeen is derived.

Going Seventeen

Going seventeen is their youtube chanel in which they upload new and interesting content every wednesday. The show is known as GoSe and it premiered on June 12 2017. It is purly for the sake of comedy and enjoyment.

It started of as a way to document behind-the-scene moments and then turned into a full fleged production. The series has it's own fan base called the "cubics"

    Popular episodes-
  1. TTT: These are episodes where the team takes short trips to nearby places and does activities to just take a break from the idol schedule.
  2. Mafia episode: These episodes have been a hit because of how crazy the game gets. The rules of the games are usually really difficult to understand but the embers get really serious about the game and about being funny.
  3. Insomnia Zero: In these episodes the challenge is for the members to talk about the embarassing or funny moments of the chosen member and whoever reacts the least gets to go gome. There are many rounds of this game. (The jokes are all made with consideration of the person's emotions)

Special info

Here are some interesting facts about Seventeen!

  1. Seventeen fans are called "CARATS"
  2. Pre-debut content was filmed in a room with green walls and thus trainee period is refered to as "green-room days"
  3. The team is divided into three units- The vocal unit, The hip-hop unit, The rap unit and each group has a leader.
  4. The members have different sub units like: The leaders unit, the 96ers(members born in 1996), the makne unit(with the three youngest members), BSS(boo-seok-soon) etc.
  5. They are known for their impeccable synchronization and that they are a self made group because since their debut they have written and produced their own music as well as choreographed their dances.
  6. Their 2023 EP FML sold over 6.2 million copies, making it the best-selling global album of the year.
  7. They are appointed as the first-ever UNESCO Goodwill Ambassadors of Youth.
